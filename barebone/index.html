Built on Ethereum infrastructure — three independent chains (Identity·AWS, Profile·Azure, Content·GCP). Uploads are hashed with SHA-256, pinned to a simulated IPFS node, and mined into immutable blocks.
  • Identity Chain (AWS) — accounts as EOAs; new accounts need email verification.
  • Profile Chain (Azure) — public creator profiles, mined separately.
  • Content Chain (GCP) — real SHA-256 + IPFS CID of every video, immutable.
